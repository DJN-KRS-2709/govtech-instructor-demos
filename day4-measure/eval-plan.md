# Eval plan · enquiry reply assistant (made up)

Made up for a live demo. Synthetic data, no real people, no real records. A learner-style file so the M12 Final Presentation Builder can load this repo.

## Who owns what

- Signs the pass bar: the enquiry desk business owner
- Writes and refreshes the test cases: enquiry desk officers
- Runs the eval: the engineering team, on every change
- Reviews a weekly sample: one named senior officer, using day4-measure/human-rubric.md

## When it runs

- Before go-live: the full set must clear the pass bar
- Before every change: model, rules or policy pages, rerun and compare
- Every week: score a sample of 20 real drafts
- When policy changes: update the affected cases first, then rerun

## How I'll know it's working

- The goal: fewer residents come back with the same enquiry
- The baseline: 1 in 4 enquiries (25%) reopened within 30 days
- The target and review date: 15% or fewer reopened within 30 days, reviewed at the end of the pilot
- A usage metric: share of drafts officers send with light edits, every week
- The pattern to watch first: officers rewriting most drafts on one enquiry type, such as permits
- The stop line: if more than 40% of drafts on one enquiry type are rewritten, pause drafts for that type

## First 30 days

- Week 1: agree the pass bar with the business owner
- Week 2: run the full set and fix the parking permit gap
- Weeks 3 and 4: start the weekly sample and report the pass rate
